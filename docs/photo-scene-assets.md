## Verified source

The primary source is the World Aquatics article
[High-Stakes Splashes in 2024: The year in diving](https://www.worldaquatics.com/news/4183036/high-stakes-splashes-in-2024-the-year-in-diving),
published 26 December 2024. The photograph appears between the account of Japan's
first Olympic diving medal and the paragraph discussing China's tenth
consecutive Olympic women's 3m springboard gold. The article's own caption names
Chen Yiwen and credits Tsutomu Kishimoto / World Aquatics.

[Original official CDN resource](https://resources.fina.org/photo-resources/2024/12/10/99384a7a-7b63-4b1a-a824-a9e758908d61/Women-s-3m-Springboard-_-Semi-Final-_-Day-13-_-Paris-2024-Olympic-Games-_Medium-Res-Image_m86274.jpg?width=1280)

The article HTML's image alt text and the CDN filename identify the **women's 3m
springboard semifinal, Day 13, Paris 2024**. Its narrative caption discusses the
eventual gold medal but does not establish that this frame came from the final.
The local filename is retained for the prototype's agreed asset path; it is not
evidence of a final-round capture.

credit, with the article source accessible. Suggested alt text: “Chen Yiwen in
mid-air during a Paris 2024 women's 3m springboard semifinal, shown as a sample

the demonstration must not claim that a recognition model established an

The downloaded file is the official CDN's width-1280 rendition; no local edits,
filters, embedded overlays, or crops were applied. Cropping in the interface
must preserve the athlete. World Aquatics is the source; no open reuse license
