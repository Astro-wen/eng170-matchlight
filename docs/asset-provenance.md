# Matchlight asset provenance

All artwork below is illustrative. It is not photography of an actual competition,
athlete, venue, or live event. No Olympic marks or event branding are included.

## Aquatics hero

- Project path: `public/images/aquatics-hero.png`
- Created: October 14, 2026.
- Method: built-in `image_gen` tool, using the imagegen skill.
- Source: AI-generated from the prompt below, with no reference images.
- Purpose: spectator dashboard aquatic-sports hero/card artwork.
- Display treatment: the product must label it as AI illustration wherever it could
  be mistaken for actual event coverage.

### Final prompt

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: landscape hero artwork for Matchlight, a welcoming spectator companion web app.
Primary request: premium editorial sports photography of a sunlit Los Angeles-inspired aquatic competition arena, emphasizing a clear turquoise competition pool and elegant white concrete diving platform with a single small adult diver on the platform for scale.
Scene/backdrop: open-air modern aquatic arena, clean sculptural architecture, a few welcoming casual spectators in distant seating, subtle palm trees beyond the arena, natural California afternoon light.
Style/medium: convincingly photographic editorial image, tactile water reflections, gentle film-like tonal contrast, clean uncluttered premium composition.
Composition/framing: wide 16:9 landscape, pool fills the lower two thirds, diving tower occupies one side, calm usable open water and sky, visually legible at small card size.
Lighting/mood: bright warm afternoon sun, calm energetic inclusive sports atmosphere.
Color palette: luminous turquoise water, warm ivory architecture, pale sky blue, restrained dark green details.
Constraints: believable geometry and water, adult athlete small in scene, all spectators distant, illustrative imagined setting rather than recreation of an actual event.
Avoid: any text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, Olympic rings or marks, flags, event branding, giant foreground people, clutter, harsh CGI rendering.
```
